# Remotage Blog Publishing & Promotion Strategy Checklist

Use this checklist template every time a new blog post is prepared for publishing.

---

## 1. Pre-Publish SEO Checks
Before hitting **"Published"** in the Remotage CMS, verify the following details:

- [ ] **Target Keyword in Title (H1)**: Ensure the primary target keyword is placed within the main heading (preferably at the beginning).
- [ ] **Keyword in Intro (First 100 Words)**: The target keyword must appear naturally in the introductory paragraph.
- [ ] **URL Slug**: Keep it short, lowercase, hyphen-separated, and matching the target keyword (e.g., `/blog/outsource-lead-generation`).
- [ ] **Subheadings (H2 / H3)**: Include the target keyword or close semantic variations in at least one `##` heading and one `###` heading.
- [ ] **Meta Title**: Limit to **55–60 characters**. Format: `Primary Keyword | Remotage`. Avoid truncation in SERPs.
- [ ] **Meta Description**: Limit to **140–160 characters**. Include a clear value proposition and a CTA (e.g., "Learn how...").
- [ ] **Image Optimization**:
  - Image files named with target keyword (e.g., `outsource-lead-generation-guide.jpg`).
  - Alt-text contains the target keyword naturally.
- [ ] **Word Count**: Verify length fits the target range (e.g. 1,200–1,500 words) to ensure depth of coverage.

---

## 2. Google Search Console (GSC) Inspection Steps
Once published, force Google to crawl and index the new URL immediately:

1. Copy the full live URL (e.g., `https://www.remotage.com/blog/how-to-outsource-lead-generation-without-losing-quality`).
2. Log into the [Google Search Console Dashboard](https://search.google.com/search-console).
3. Paste the URL into the **"URL Inspection"** search bar at the top of GSC and press Enter.
4. Click **"Test Live URL"** to verify there are no mobile-usability or schema rendering issues.
5. If the live test passes, click **"Request Indexing"** to queue the URL for immediate indexing.

## 4. Internal Linking Checklist
To maximize search authority, connect all new articles to relevant service pages:

- **Link Destination**: Every blog post must include 2–3 links with descriptive anchor texts linking back to the relevant service pages or sections (e.g., [lead generation services](https://www.remotage.com/services)).
- **Anchor Text Best Practices**:
  - **Avoid**: "Click here", "our page", "read more".
  - **Use**: "B2B lead generation specialists", "outsource virtual bookkeeping", "customer support outsourcing".
- **Reverse Internal Linking**: After publishing a new blog post, find 2–3 *existing* pages or older blog posts and insert links pointing to the *newly published* post to pass link equity.
